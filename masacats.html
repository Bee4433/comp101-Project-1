Five cats. Two families. Five unique personalities.

Masa's Cats

Ame

Ame is a four-year-old brown tabby who lives with Masa's family. He is the oldest of three brothers and has a big appetite. His vet says he is overweight.

Ame's Favourite

  • Sleeping
  • Eating
  • Play with his brothers

$

$ is a two-year-old orange tabby and the youngest of the three brothers. He is very shy around people and usually keeps his distance, but sometimes he becomes surprisingly cuddly. Masa's mom named him $ because she thinks of the dollar as a strong currency.

$'s Favourite

  • Playing
  • Sleeping
  • Walk around the back yard

Travis

Travis is a three-year-old orange tabby with a gentle personality. He never scratches or bites and loves playing with his brothers. He is shy around strangers, but affectionate with his family.

Travis's Favourite

  • Chasing
  • Climbing
  • Watching birds

Ivan's Cats

Kiwi

Kiwi is an affectionate cat who lives with Ivan's family. He is Aurora's older brother and loves to eat, sometimes even trying to eat things that aren't food. He also enjoys climbing to high places, watching his surroundings, and taking naps.

Kiwi's Favourite

  • Sleeping
  • Eating
  • Nuzzling

Aurora

Aurora is Kiwi's sister. Ivan's family took them in together as stray cats. She is friendly but cautious and always keeps an eye on her surroundings. She prefers quiet, safe places where she can eat and sleep comfortably.

Aurora's favourite

  • Hide
  • Sleeping
  • Looking out the window
